Current method

Denoising steps 30–34 run with the video latent at half height and half width. Before downsampling, the full discrete cosine transform (DCT) spectrum is saved. When returning to full resolution, the saved high-frequency component is scaled according to the change in denoising noise level (σ) and smoothly blended back over eight frequency bands. Unlike the earlier version, no random high-frequency noise is generated.

Decision gate

Reject if the candidate introduces visible blur, artifacts, flicker, noticeable changes in subject, motion, camera trajectory, prompt adherence, or audible defects. Numerical metrics such as structural similarity (SSIM) and peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) are diagnostic only and do not override human review.
